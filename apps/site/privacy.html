Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft. This policy describes how the app works today. It must be reviewed, given a real contact address, and hosted on a verified domain before public release.

The short version

Email Organizer is a desktop application. It runs on your computer and talks directly to Google. We operate no server that receives, stores, or processes your email.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant the app permission to read and modify your Gmail (the gmail.modify scope). The app uses it to:

  • read message metadata: sender, subject, date, labels, size, and unsubscribe headers;
  • add or remove labels, archive messages, move messages to Trash, and mark messages read or unread when you apply a rule or undo a change.

The app does not read the body of your emails or send email. It moves mail to Gmail’s Trash only when you apply a rule that says so; Gmail itself permanently deletes Trash after 30 days, and you can undo before then.

Where data lives

  • On your computer only: message metadata fetched for scanning is held in memory during your session. Your rules and activity log are stored in a local database. Your Google sign-in token is encrypted using your operating system’s keychain.
  • Not on our servers: none of this is sent to us or to any third party.

Unsubscribing

If you choose to unsubscribe from a sender, the app sends a request directly from your computer to the web address the sender published in its email, or opens that page or a pre-filled email for you.

Google API Services disclosure

Email Organizer’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Gmail is used only to provide the user-facing features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not transferred to others.

Your choices

  • Click Disconnect in the app to revoke access and delete the stored sign-in from your computer.
  • Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes your local rules and log.
