How it works

Four steps, and you stay in control the whole way.

Sign in with Google

Your browser opens Google’s own sign-in page. We never see your password.

Scan

The app reads message headers (sender, subject, date, labels) on your computer and shows what’s taking over your inbox.

Preview

Pick or write a rule and see exactly which messages it would touch. Nothing changes yet.

Apply, or undo

Apply the rule when you’re happy. Every change is logged and can be undone.

What it does

Practical tools for the mess, not another inbox to check.

Inbox overview

See your top senders, how much is unread, and what categories your mail falls into.

Automatic categories

Receipts, travel, newsletters, promotions, social, and finance are recognized from message headers, with no need to read your message text.

Rules you control

Label, archive, trash, or mark as read based on sender, domain, subject, category, age, or unread status.

Preview and undo

Every rule shows what it would change before it runs, and each run can be reversed from the activity log.

Unsubscribe helper

Find the senders you never read. Unsubscribe in one click when the sender supports it, or auto-archive their mail instead.

Free up space safely

Rules can move old clutter to Trash, with the size shown before you confirm. Gmail keeps Trash for 30 days, so you can undo. The app never permanently deletes anything itself.

Private by design

The app runs on your computer. There is no Email Organizer server that holds your mail.

  • Your mail goes straight from Google to your computer. We don’t receive, store, or see it.
  • The app reads message headers only, not the body of your emails.
  • Your sign-in is encrypted with your system’s keychain, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • No ads, no selling data, no analytics on your mail.

Questions

Does anyone see my email?

No. The app talks directly between your computer and Google. We run no server that receives your mail.

What permission does it ask Google for?

Permission to read and modify your Gmail so it can apply labels and archive. It does not ask to send mail or delete it permanently.

Can I undo changes?

Yes. Each rule run is logged, and you can undo it from the Activity tab.

Which providers and systems work?

Gmail on macOS is first. Outlook and other platforms are planned.

How do I remove access?

Click Disconnect in the app, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
